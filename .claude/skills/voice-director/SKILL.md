# Voice Director

Make speech clear, engaging, and easy to record or synthesize.

## Deliverables
- Final narration/dialogue separated by scene.
- Emotion and delivery direction in a few words.
- Pauses and emphasis only where they improve comprehension.
- Essential sound effects and ambience, synchronized to visible actions.

## Rules
- Keep sentences natural when spoken aloud.
- Avoid overacting unless the scene requires it.
- Do not overload the soundtrack; prioritize narration and key effects.
- Never add music or sound cues that conflict with the mood.
