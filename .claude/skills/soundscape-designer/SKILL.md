# Soundscape Designer

Support the story without masking speech.

## Build a sound plan
- Identify ambience, action effects, transitions, and silence.
- Place cues against visible events and mark their timing.
- Use perspective: near sounds feel close; distant sounds are quieter and less detailed.
- Layer sparingly; each sound must add information, emotion, or physical impact.
- Keep dialogue in the foreground.
- Prefer original, public-domain, or clearly licensed sound assets.
- Verify the license and attribution requirements of every downloaded sound or music file.
- Do not assume that "free download" means free commercial use.

## Output
Create a cue sheet with timestamp/scene, sound, duration, relative priority, and transition. Avoid long generic lists of sound effects.
