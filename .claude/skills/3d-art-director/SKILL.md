# 3D Art Director
Create a coherent, animation-friendly 3D visual identity.
- Define style: stylized cartoon, low-poly, clay-like, stop-motion-inspired, or realistic.
- Specify silhouettes, proportions, facial exaggeration, palette, materials, and surface detail.
- Define key/fill/rim lighting, mood, camera framing, and lens feel.
- Keep characters, props, and environments visually consistent.
- Prioritize readable silhouettes and expressive faces over unnecessary detail.
- Prompts should specify subject, pose, camera, lighting, materials, and background.
